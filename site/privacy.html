2. What the extension reads, and why

When you press Export on a LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Recruiter search, the extension reads the search results already displayed in your browser and assembles them into a spreadsheet. It runs only on linkedin.com, only when you start an export, and it is read-only: it does not send messages, connection requests, or change anything in your LinkedIn account.

We do not receive or store the candidate data you export. It goes from your browser into your file or your Google Sheet.

3. Account data we hold

Your email address, your plan, and counts of how many rows you have exported. That is the whole list. We do not store your name, your company, your searches, or the candidates you found.

4. Email lookups (Pro)

If you turn on "Find & verify emails", the extension sends a candidate's first name, last name and company name to our server, which forwards them to our enrichment provider and returns an email address. We do not log the request or the response, and we do not keep either. The enrichment provider is an independent controller with its own policy: [PROVIDER NAME + POLICY LINK — human task, Phase 6].

5. Payments

Payments are handled by DodoPayments as Merchant of Record. Card details never reach us. We receive a customer id, a subscription id, and a status.

6. Health statistics

The extension sends anonymous counters after an export: how many rows, how many pages, what fraction of fields it managed to read, and which version of our selector configuration was used. These contain no candidate data, no names, no emails, no URLs and no search terms. They exist so we notice LinkedIn changing their page layout before you do. You can turn them off in the extension's settings.

7. Google Sheets

If you push to Google Sheets, the extension asks Google for permission to write to your spreadsheets. That token stays in your browser and is never sent to our servers. We ask for the spreadsheets scope only.

9. Your rights

Email us to see, correct, export or delete what we hold. Deleting your account removes your user row and your subscription row. Because we never hold candidate data, there is nothing on our side for a candidate to request — that data only ever existed in your browser and your own files.

We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
