What it does

  • One-click export of the search results you are already looking at.
  • Verified work emails on Pro, with a per-row status: verified, risky or not found.
  • Greenhouse and Lever presets, plus a generic format you can edit column by column.
  • Dedupe against everything you have exported before, so you stop re-sourcing the same people.
  • Google Sheets push straight into a sheet you own.

Account-safe by design

This is the part most tools hide. We page through your results at reading speed with randomized pauses, cap the day at 1,000 rows, and stop immediately if LinkedIn shows an unusual-activity check. The extension is read-only: it never sends messages, never sends connection requests, and never changes anything on LinkedIn.

It never asks for your LinkedIn password or your cookies. It works inside the session you are already signed into, in your own browser.

We will not pretend this makes you immune to anything. LinkedIn's terms restrict automated collection, and using any tool in this category is at your own risk. What we can promise is that we do not push your account harder than a fast human would.

Agency recruiters and sourcers who run several searches a week and lose two hours a time to copy-paste, email guessing and column cleanup.
